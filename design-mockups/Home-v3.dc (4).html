Full methodology
JOURNAL OF FINANCE · 2012
Decoding Inside Information
Cohen, Malloy & Pomorski
Insiders who trade outside their usual calendar pattern earn significant abnormal returns. Those on predictable schedules earn essentially none.
How Sentra uses it: the core classification. Every insider is judged against their own history.
REVIEW OF ECONOMICS & STATISTICS · 2003
Estimating the Returns to Insider Trading
Jeng, Metrick & Zeckhauser
Insider purchases earn roughly 6% abnormal return over six months. Insider sales show no significant predictive power.
How Sentra uses it: only purchases are scored. Sales are too noisy to mean much.
